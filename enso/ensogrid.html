El Niño and La Niña: Climatology and Impacts — global grid

← extremewx.org · What happens around the world in El Niño and La Niña years, compared with a normal year. Pick El Niño, La Niña or the normal Climatology, and a variable; choose the whole year, a season or a single month with the bar above the map. An ENSO year runs from June to May (1997–1998 = June 1997 – May 1998), the twelve months over which an event usually grows, peaks and decays. Hover over the map to read the values. More options are under Advanced settings. These maps describe past years, not a forecast: for NOAA’s official U.S. outlook for the coming months, see the CPC seasonal outlooks, and for the El Niño or La Niña under way now, CPC’s ENSO discussion.

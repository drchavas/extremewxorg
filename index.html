Extreme Weather: Maps and Trends

by Dan Chavas · Professor · Purdue University, Department of Earth, Atmospheric, and Planetary Sciences

Interactive maps and explorers for extreme weather. Many things a work in progress, new updates expected regularly. Email me if there is a feature you would like to see added!

Tropical Cyclones

Historical Event Viewer
  • IBTrACS TC Track & Wind Footprint Map Explorer ↗ external site (tcviewer.org) Any Atlantic storm since 1851 — best-track by Saffir–Simpson category with 34/64 kt wind swaths; click any point for full data.
Historical Trends
  • Climatology & Trends — 5° grid Paired climatology and trend maps on a 5° global grid, each with its zonal profile alongside, plus annual, seasonal and month-by-month series. Track density, storm count, ACE, intensity, central pressure and storm size, by basin, 1980–2025 from IBTrACS. Click a basin to zoom in.

Severe Weather (wind, hail, tornado, derecho, freezing rain)

Historical Event Viewer
  • Historical Severe Weather Events Maps of any severe thunderstorm and derecho day from the historical record
Historical Trends
  • Climatology & Trends — county level Paired climatology and trend maps at U.S. county resolution, with annual and month-by-month series. Hail, tornado, thunderstorm wind, freezing rain and peak wind.
  • Climatology & Trends — 2° grid The same paired maps binned into a regular 2° grid.
